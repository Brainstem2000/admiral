Two Calls On The Ore one lock removed, one lock imposed
Unlocked — the BoM bug 36,027 units wrongly marked unsellable You asked why Morg was reporting titanium and iron as BoM-locked. A hardcoded list in galaxy-market.ts had told every agent for about a week that five ore types were reserved for a commission. It now derives from commission_requirements — and owning the hull retires its bill, so a delivered commission stops reserving stock. Juno's caravan was already built and still holding its own materials hostage.
Locked — your call 7,222 fury_crystal held off the market Nova was mid-liquidation when you said we might need fury crystal for ship upgrades and commissions. She had sold 376; 5,109 went straight back into storage. You were right, and more so than this board first claimed. It is a build material in 47 crimson hulls, and every remaining Devastator line except one runs through it.

The pattern worth keeping Both of these were stale reservations — stock held back for a reason that had expired or was never checked. The first cost the fleet a week of unsellable ore. The second would have cost the Devastator. The fix in both cases was the same: derive the lock from live state, never from a list someone wrote once.
