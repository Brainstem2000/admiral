Why nine of twelve were refuted The patterns to check before acting on any future ship analysis.
01
Cost priced from catalog base_value, not live asks Base value is not what you pay. Hit Rook, Vera and Cass — three of the nine.
02
Modules with zero ask depth in the agent's own empire Unbuyable at any price, however good the plan reads. Cass's afterburner had no asks anywhere in Crimson space.
03
"Does it fix the binding limit" never actually checked CyberSapper's 378,468cr Survey Vessel was a downgrade on his own stated constraint — utility slots.
04
Crew requirements ignored The standout. Zibal was recommended a 202,737cr junk_convoy requiring a minimum crew of 60 — against his crew of 1. A hull he could not have flown.
60 crew required vs 1 crew aboard
05
Station assumptions asserted from stale position data Several agents had moved or were in transit. The surveying agents caught this and corrected it rather than inventing a board — read position before routing.
Commands worth recording Purchase is buy_listed_ship(listing_id=…) — there is no buy_ship.
browse_ships is per-station but accepts an explicit base_id, which works undocked.
commission_quote prices the caller's current station and takes no base_id — to price a yard, an agent must stand in it.
