Three rules the planner encodes
A slot you cannot power is not a slot The budget is free capacity PLUS what the swapped-out module returns — not free capacity alone.
special is role-critical gas_harvesting only harvests gas, so a higher-power laser is a downgrade for a gas rig. common_only strip miners take only iron and copper.
Never strip the last harvester An early scoring pass rated a +4 afterburner level with a 40-power laser and proposed exactly that.
mining_laser_v fits nobody It needs 13 CPU / 24 power and the largest headroom on any hull in the fleet is 10. CyberSpock alone has the budget — but it would cost him gas harvesting entirely, so he takes gas_harvester_iv instead. The 103,000cr earmarked for it is saved, not transferred.
